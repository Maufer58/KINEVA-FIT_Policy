2. Nature of the service

The application is published as KINEVA FIT (formerly MOVE Fitness / M.O.V.E.) on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

KINEVA FIT is a fitness app (running, gym and Apple Watch training) and workout-planning tool, including multi-client features for trainers/gyms. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or medical advice.

User- or trainer-entered “focus areas” / physical limitations and self-declared conditions are training preferences / declarations, not clinical records. If such data qualify as health data (GDPR Art. 9), processing is based on explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), collected via in-app acceptance flows and system permissions.

4. Purposes

  1. Provide fitness features (routines, timers, history, export, Watch).
  2. Manage athlete/client profiles and training preferences (including multi-trainer).
  3. Sync workouts with Apple Watch when enabled.
  4. Write completed workouts to Apple Health / Fitness (iOS, if authorised).
  5. Measure distance, pace and route for outdoor runs (Athlete Pro+).
  6. Show the street map of the route after a session.
  7. Manage subscriptions, device seats, AI credits and add-ons (e.g. Pilates pack).
  8. Protect trainer area / App Lock (PIN, device biometrics).
  9. Local technical diagnostics for stability and security.
  10. Legal compliance.

We do not sell personal data to brokers or run third-party advertising profiling. There is no KINEVA FIT application backend that centralises routines, GPS or biometrics.

5. Storage

  • Offline-first: most data stays on device (AES-encrypted Hive DB; Keychain/Keystore where applicable).
  • GPS coordinates and the route track stay on device (and Apple Watch if used). They are not uploaded to KINEVA FIT servers.
  • Map providers may receive only the geographic bounding box needed to render streets.
  • Backup/export/sync only when you start them (File / Email / WhatsApp / ZIP to Files/iCloud/Drive) or when a feature requires it (e.g. WatchConnectivity).
  • Licence seat registry may sync via iCloud Keychain if enabled (licence metadata only, not workout routines).
  • Retention: until in-app deletion, uninstall, or erasure request; store billing records remain with Apple / Google per their policies.

9. Security & confidentiality

Measures include: encrypted local Hive database (key in Keychain/Keystore); trainer PIN and recovery code stored as hashes; App Lock with optional device biometrics; selective routine sharing that excludes email, phone, conditions and full biometrics (id + name only), unless you run an explicit full trainer transfer/export; no KINEVA FIT app server for routines/GPS; cleartext HTTP disabled on Android.

No system is 100% secure. Protect the device and do not share unencrypted backups. If you share exports via email/WhatsApp, you control that transmission.
